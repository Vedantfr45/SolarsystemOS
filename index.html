Orrery
Heliocentric positions
Made by Vedant, and a little bit Claude

Every body sits at its true direction from the Sun for the date shown, computed from JPL orbital elements. Distances and sizes are compressed so the whole system fits on one screen. Earth and Moon surfaces are image maps based on NASA data, via the three.js examples, and the star backdrop is a real panorama of the Milky Way sky. The asteroid belt, the galaxies and the cosmic web are generated models: galaxy directions follow real measurements with distances compressed, and the belt is drawn far denser than it is.
